# Dynasty Year 19 Rulebook

***Overview***

**Goal** -  Mimic the real MLB as much as possible with a closed (no in-season tampering needed) rulebook.

**Cost** -  $200. Send money to banker (Alan C) via paypal.

**Payout** -   Top 3 places, 70%, 20%, 10%

**Draft** **Time** -  **Determined yearly**

**Draft Budget** - $400 base budget (before contracts and cash trades)

**Salary Cap** **-** In season salary cap of $530. If you go over the salary cap, you will not score any points until you drop back below by dropping/trading players

**Roster** **Spots** - 23 Active spots -  Acquired at auction and must fill the following positions:

• Offense (14) – C, C, 1B, 2B, SS, 3B, MI, CI, OF, OF, OF, OF, OF, UTIL

• Pitching (9) – Any combination of RP and SP, 9 total pitchers

• Last  year's  champ  nominates  first,  the  rest  of  the  nomination  order  is  random.

• 7 Bench spots - Acquired at snake draft following auction. Default pick is highest ranked Fantrax player available. Draft order is reverse regular season standings from last year.  There is no position requirement for the snake draft.  All are $0.

• 2 Farm spots - Acquired in non-snake draft following auction and reserve draft. Default pick is highest available Baseball America  'Top  100  Prospects'  player.  The draft order is (postseason standings): 7th, 9th, 11th, 8th, 10th 12th, 13th, 5th, 4th, 6th, 14th, 3rd, 2nd, 1st

• Unlimited IL spots - You may draft or an injured player and place them on your IL, and you may trade for a player already on the IL, but otherwise you may place an injured player on your IL only if they appeared in at least one regular season game while on your roster before the IL placement. For example you can't waiver claim an IL player and stash him on your IL. See Giancarlo Stanton before 2025 regular season - clearly injured as stated by team and Fantrax but not on IL. Around trades: If a trade is made involving an injured player, the acquiring team may only place that player on the IL if the trading team was also eligible to.

• You have 4 days to get a healthy player off the DL, after that Fantrax enforces an illegal roster. (No stats for offense.)

**Stat** **Categories** -

Batting (8) - R, HR, RBI, SB, AVG, 2B, 3B, BB

Pitching (8) -   W, K, SV, ERA, WHIP, HOLDS, BB/9 IP, K/9 IP

**IP** **minimum** -   44 IP, except Championship week, which has a 66 IP minimum

**Missed IP penalty** -  1-2 wks before playoffs: $20, 3-4 weeks before: $15, 5-6 weeks before $10, before All-Star Break: $5. Automatic loss for ERA, WHIP, BB/9, K/9 for Missed IP

**Waivers** - A dropped player is ineligible to be bid on at the next silent auction or picked up in the same week (M-Su) as he was dropped, by the owner that dropped him.

From week 10 onwards, the 'Special  Waivers'  system  will  be  in  place  to  counteract   dumping or dropping very good players. See  '**Special Waivers**'  below for details on this.

**Playoff Structure:**

- 7 teams in champions bracket and 6 in consolation bracket. 14th place in regular season do not qualify for playoffs
  - Bye for 1st ranked team in playoff bracket and the 8th/9th ranked teams in the consolation bracket
  - Championship week is 2 weeks long and will conclude one week before the end of the regular season to avoid late season load management in MLB playoff teams

---

**Free Agents:**  Anyone at any level is eligible.

---

**Trades:**    Offseason trading is allowed up until the auction, after all new and old owners have    chosen their contracts. Regular season trading is allowed until trade deadline, which is generally 1 week after the MLB deadline

**Eligibility** - Regular season trades may involve 1. Free agents, 2. Contracts, 3. FAAB, 4. Buy-out contracts,  5. Next 5 years of farms draft picks.

Offseason trades may involve 1. Contracts, 2. Draft budget, 3.  Next 2 years of farms draft picks.

**Vetos** -   Each trade will have a 2+ day waiting period.  1 veto vote brings a league-wide vote, where *everyone* must vote on trade.

---

**Contracts:**  Owners may have up to 10 players signed to active contracts going into the auction. The only exception is if more have been acquired via sign and trade between contract sheet signing and auction.

• Contracts are paid for at the draft.

• Retained farm players do not count against the 10 contract limit.

**The** **Formula** - All prices are  *per* *year*.

• 1 year = the original acquisition price, whether at the draft or from FAAB;

• 2 years = original price + $5;

• 3 years = original price + $10;

• 4 years = original price + $15;

• 5+ years = original price + $4\*(number of years)

• When any contract is dropped or expires the player just becomes a free agent.

**Buyouts** -   If an owner wants to drop a long term contract, he *must* buyout the contract at a rate of 80% of the price for the 1st unpaid year, 70% for the next year, 60% the next year, 50%, 40%..., and so on.

• A player in the last year of his contract can be dropped with no penalty.  (He was already paid for at the draft.)

**Farm System Overview**:

**Eligibility** - Only players who have yet to log an MLB plate appearance or pitch thrown are farm-eligible.

• Players do not need to be in a minor league system to be drafted (i.e. NPL, College, or High School players are available)

**Default** **Picks** -  The highest ranked farm-eligible player as determined by Baseball America's  'Top  100  Preseason Rookies'  listing.

**Salary** -   Each pick has a base salary of $1, taken out of the draft budget.

**Retention** - A player may be retained on the farm for additional years. The cost depends on MLB playing time:

• $1 added to base salary - If the player has not yet appeared in an MLB game.

• $2 added to base salary - If the player has officially appeared in an MLB game.

• Retention decisions for these players are made at contract signing time.

**Add** **Period** - The only time a player can be placed on a farm team is via the 2 picks allotted at the draft, or via trade.

**Roster** **size** -  There is no maximum farm roster size.

**Promoting** - A farm player can be promoted to the active fantasy roster anytime, regardless of MLB experience.

• Once a farm player is called up, he can never be sent back down

**Deadbeat rule:** We will have a disincentive for falling off the map. This will be in the form of an additional up-front "deposit" to make sure we don't have deadbeat owners. For all non-deadbeat owners, the deposit will be returned; kept deposits will be added to the winnings pool.

• The deadbeat deposit will be $50 per team.

• There are two triggers for losing that deposit:

a. A player who is placed on the (real) disabled list is left in an active roster spot for one calendar week [i.e. 7 days]

b. An owner misses their weekly IP minimum for 3 or more scoring periods in a season.

• **Longer off-season trading period:** Trades will be allowed after the season has ended up until a contracts signing and administrative blackout period, THE LENGTH OF WHICH IS DECIDED BY THE COMMISSIONER (No longer than 1 week).  I will work out the logistics of timelines as we get the season going, but essentially the blackout period will allow me to reset the contract sheet, send it to everyone, sign contracts, reset budgets, do next season calculations, etc.
