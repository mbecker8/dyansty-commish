Help

Dynasty Commish replaces the contract-signing spreadsheet. Fantrax still runs everything else: trades, the auction and drafts, lineups, waivers and scoring. This site handles the parts done by hand outside Fantrax: contracts, buyouts, the farm system, and the auction budget math.

Signing in

  • Click Sign in with Discord and use the Discord account you use in the league server. The site only learns your Discord ID and username. It never sees your email, servers or messages.
  • If you see “Your Discord account isn't linked to a team”, send the commissioner the Discord ID shown on that page. Once they've linked it, sign in again.
  • Sign out from the button in the top right. If you're removed from a team, you lose access on the next page you open.

What you can see

Every manager can see every team. Nothing here is private to a team.

  • Teams: each team's {{ next_season }} auction budget: the $400 base, minus contracts, buyouts, farm salaries and missed-IP penalties, plus or minus cash trades. This is what's already committed. The contracts you sign and the farm players you keep in February come out of it.
  • Your team page: your contracts, buyouts, farm, farm picks and cash trades. Open How it adds up to see every charge behind the budget.
  • Contracts: every contract that runs through {{ next_season }} or later, with its price per year, final year and years left.
  • Buyouts: dropped contracts that still owe penalties, season by season.
  • Farm: every farm system, with what each player would cost to keep next season.
  • Farm picks: who owns each future farm draft pick. Bold means it was traded.
  • Cash: auction money traded between teams, and which auction it applies to.

A yellow banner at the top means the commissioner is still reviewing moves from the {{ league_season }} season (trades, drops, promotions). Until that's done, rosters and budgets may still change.

Signing ({{ next_season }})

When the commissioner opens signing, a Signing link appears at the top. Your page has three parts, and the budget box on the right updates as you change anything.

  • Sign players: every player on your roster who can get a new contract, with the price per year for each length. The price comes from his end-of-{{ league_season }} Fantrax salary. Players under contract, whose contract just ended, or who need the commissioner's attention are listed at the bottom with the reason.
  • Buy out a contract: tick a contract to drop it. You'll see every penalty it leaves, season by season. Buying out frees a contract spot.
  • Farm: choose Keep or Release for every farm player. Keeping one costs $1 more than his salary, or $2 more once he's appeared in MLB.

Save draft keeps your choices without sending them. Submit sends them to the commissioner. It only works once there are no problems listed (e.g. more than 10 contracts, or a budget below $0) and every farm player is decided. To change something after submitting, click Withdraw submission, edit, and submit again. Nothing is final until the commissioner locks signing.

Your choices are private to your team and the commissioners until signing locks. After that, every team's new contracts and auction budget appear on the league pages.

Export downloads the league (budgets, contracts, buyouts, farms, picks, cash) as CSV files for a spreadsheet.

The rules in brief

  • Contract price per year (P = end-of-season salary): 1 year P; 2 years P+$5; 3 years P+$10; 4 years P+$15; 5 or more years P + $4 × years.
  • Contract term: signed after season S for L years, it covers seasons S+1 to S+L. The final year is S+L.
  • Buyouts: dropping a contract before its final year costs 80% of the annual price at the next auction, then 70%, 60%, … for each season left, rounded half up. The team holding the contract when he's dropped pays. A final-year drop is free.
  • Farm: a pick costs $1. Keeping a player adds $1 to his salary, or $2 once he has appeared in MLB (1 plate appearance or 0.1 innings pitched). Once called up, a farm player can't go back down.
  • Limit: up to 10 contracts at signing. Kept farm players don't count. Sign-and-trades happen in Fantrax after signing, by gentlemen's agreement, so the app doesn't count them.

The full rulebook is the league's Google Doc.

Commissioner guide

You see this section because you're a commissioner. Everything below happens in the admin.

Link a manager to a team

  1. Ask them to sign in once. The page they land on shows their Discord ID. (Or, in Discord, turn on Developer Mode, right-click their name and choose Copy User ID.)
  2. Admin → Managers → Add: name, team, and the numeric Discord ID. Tick Is commissioner for co-commissioners.
  3. They sign in again. A team can have more than one manager.
  • Remove access: clear the Discord ID or delete the Manager. They're locked out on their next page.
  • Hand a team to someone new: change the Discord ID. The new person gets their own account. Nothing carries over from the old one.
  • Commissioner rights follow the Is commissioner box. Admin access is granted or removed on that person's next page load.

Fantrax moves (sync)

The app reads every Fantrax trade, drop and claim, plus promotions and MLB debuts from the rosters, and applies them itself. Each move is applied once. Press Sync from Fantrax on the console whenever you want the records caught up; running it again only picks up what's new.

  • What it applies: a trade moves the contract or farm player; a drop before the contract's final year creates the buyout, owed by the team that dropped him; a drop in the final year voids the contract with no penalty; a farm drop releases him; a farm player in an active roster slot is promoted; a farm player's first plate appearance or out records his MLB debut. Claims change nothing. A claimed player has no contract until he's signed.
  • Exceptions are the few things it can't sort out: a move that contradicts the records, a Minors player who isn't on a farm (usually a draft pick to add), MLB games with no plate appearance or out, a trade comment that mentions cash, or a contract whose Fantrax roster doesn't match. They're listed on the console. Fix the data by hand (usually in the admin), then resolve the exception with a note saying what you did. Signing can't open while any are listed.
  • If the app applied something wrongly (a trade that was later reversed, say), correct the contract, buyout or farm player in the admin. Admin edits are audited, and the next sync won't undo them.
  • After renewal, offseason trades happen in the new Fantrax league. Use Find new Fantrax leagues on the console to add it; the sync then reads both.
  • Fantrax login: the sync uses your browser's Fantrax cookie (FANTRAX_COOKIE on the server, secrets/fantrax_cookie.txt locally). When it expires, the console says so. Copy a fresh Cookie header from a logged-in Fantrax tab and update it.
  • Every change is listed under Moves this season on each team's page, and in full in Admin → Fantrax events.

Run the signing period

Everything here is on the commissioner console (Commish in the menu).

  1. Sync from Fantrax once offseason trades stop, and resolve every exception. The console won't open signing while any are listed.
  2. Load the rosters: manage.py sync_rosters --fantrax data/fantrax/<blackout>. Prices always come from the end-of-season snapshot (--salaries, 2026-final by default). The command lists any contract whose player isn't on his team's roster. That's a drop or trade the sync hasn't applied yet. Rosters can be re-loaded until signing opens, and not after.
  3. Open signing on the console. Optionally set a deadline in Admin → Signing periods. It's shown to managers, but nothing locks automatically.
  4. Watch the teams table: status, new contracts, buyouts, contract count, budget left and any problems. Review / edit opens a team's page. Changing another team's decisions needs a note, which goes in the audit log. You can edit a team even after it has submitted. If your edit leaves problems, it goes back to a draft and the team has to resubmit.
  5. Lock signing when every team is ready. Locking applies every team's saved decisions in one go: new contracts (signed {{ league_season }}), buyouts (dropped in {{ league_season }}), farm keeps (salary bumped for {{ next_season }}) and releases. It's refused if any team has a problem or an undecided farm player. A team that hasn't submitted gets its saved draft applied. Locking can't be undone from the console.

The audit log records every save, submit, withdraw, open and lock, every Fantrax sync and resolved exception, and every add, change or delete in the admin.

Enter things by hand

  • Cash trades: Admin → Cash trades. The budget season is the auction the money applies to. Enter the Fantrax trade ID too when there is one: a trade comment that mentions cash then stops being flagged.
  • Farm draft picks: Admin → Farm players ($1, drafted this season). Until you add one, the sync flags him as a Minors player who isn't on a farm.
  • Missed-IP penalties: Admin → Budget adjustments, kind “Missed IP penalty”, the season it's charged to, and a note (e.g. the week).
  • Farm pick trades: Admin → Farm picks, then change the owner.
  • Buyouts are normally created by the Fantrax sync or at signing. The drop season must be before the contract's final year.
  • Missing salary: a roster player with no end-of-season salary can't be signed. Set it in Admin → Roster entries. Once signing opens, only a missing salary can be filled in; set ones are fixed, so a team's submitted prices can't change under it.

Command line (on the machine that runs the site)

  • bin/local-review.sh: run the site locally on its own database (review.sqlite3), keeping your decisions. It reads Discord credentials from secrets/discord.env.
  • uv run python scripts/fantrax_snapshot.py <league id> data/fantrax/<name>: take a fresh Fantrax snapshot (needs the Fantrax cookie in secrets/fantrax_cookie.txt).
  • uv run python manage.py sync_fantrax: the same as the console button. Add --snapshot data/fantrax/<name> to read a saved snapshot instead (with --league <id> once a season has two Fantrax leagues), and --dry-run to see what would change without saving it.
  • uv run python manage.py sync_rosters --fantrax data/fantrax/<name>: load the signing pool from the blackout snapshot, before signing opens.
  • uv run python manage.py import_league: first-time load of the Year 19 sheet and the 2026 Fantrax snapshot. It refuses to overwrite data once you've made decisions, admin edits or applied Fantrax events.
